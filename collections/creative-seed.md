# Open Shelf — Creative Seed

## Story — The Reading Room With No Locked Ending
A small reading room welcomed fragments: a sketch, a tune, a field note, a question. The keeper arranged them by theme but never pretended they all came from one hand. Every shelf had a card explaining what was known, what was inferred, and what remained open.

## Art direction — Modular reading room
A simple architectural illustration with four shelves—STORY, IMAGE, SOUND, DATA—and a central index. Each item has a small provenance card. Make the room inviting and modular rather than monumental.

## Music sketch — “Open Shelf” (original)
A light 4/4 folk motif at 96 BPM: C–G–E–G | D–A–F–A | E–B–G–B | G–D–B–G. Use acoustic plucks and hand percussion; keep it entirely original.
